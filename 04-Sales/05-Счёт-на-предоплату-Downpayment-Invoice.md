An A/R Down Payment Invoice is an accounting document that creates a posting to the advances account. The created invoice can later be used to settle an A/R Invoice.

## Creating an A/R Down Payment Invoice

The invoice is created using the Copy From function.  
To create a new invoice:

1. Open the document from Main Menu → Sales - A/R → A/R Down Payment Invoice.

   <img src="../.assets/04713983-cb5a-4530-81f4-8f332dec3d22.png" alt="" width="100%" />

2. Complete the document Header:

(1) Customer Code or Name  
(2) Invoice Date. In this case, Due Date means the latest date by which payment must be received.  

 <img src="../.assets/11c8665f-3846-49f4-8bdb-a0ae0ee1899b.png" alt="" width="100%" />

3. Click [Copy From] and select [Sales Order] from the drop-down list.

<img src="../.assets/f526998b-a5a1-43c6-a8e9-dca9bda0e9e7.png" alt="" width="100%" />

4. A list of open Sales Orders is displayed. Select the required document(s) and click [Choose].

5. Use the Draw Document Wizard to transfer data from the Sales Order into the Down Payment Invoice.

   <img src="../.assets/9c5fce5e-988e-4d3e-a35c-18db8e4632f4.png" alt="" width="100%" />
<img src="../.assets/e08df9e9-1bc3-401b-b488-2ed9e968a549.png" alt="" width="100%" />

6. Verify the data in the document table.

7. Complete the lower part of the form.

(1) If a partial amount is required, specify either the percentage of the full Sales Order amount in the Percentage field or the amount directly in the Amount field.  
(2) Select your signature in the Owner field.  
(3) Click [Add] to save/post the document.  
<img src="../.assets/a1c2f097-b990-40bb-9f5a-ac8c8b75c7bb.png" alt="" width="100%" />
