A Sales Blanket Agreement is an optional document in the sales process. However, if an agreement actually exists, it should also be recorded in SAP. Using a Blanket Agreement allows data to be aggregated in one form and helps monitor fulfillment of the agreed conditions.  
In SAP Business One, a Blanket Agreement can use two main agreement methods:  
Item - an agreement to supply specific items at an agreed price and quantity by a specified date. After the agreement is created, the item positions cannot be changed.  
Monetary - an agreement to supply items within an amount agreed with the Customer. The agreement amount can be divided into periods (for example, EGP 10,000 per month for one year).

## Creating a Sales Blanket Agreement

To create a Sales Blanket Agreement in the System:

1. Open Main Menu → Sales - A/R → Sales Blanket Agreement.

 <img src="../.assets/fa445f56-5c69-4b19-9395-18d3adbaf26e.png" alt="" width="100%" />

2. The System opens the Blanket Agreement form in Find Mode. Press [Ctrl] + [A] (English keyboard layout) to switch the form to Add Mode.

   \

3. Complete the Agreement Header.

(1) Specify the Business Partner.  
(2) Verify the Blanket Agreement numbering series.  
(3) Select the [Monetary] agreement method.  
(4) Specify the Agreement Start Date and End Date.  
(5) Enter the Agreement Number.  
(6) Select the Agreement Currency. The selected currency will be **used in all subsequent documents**.  
(7) Verify and, if necessary, adjust the Agreement Signing Date.  

 <img src="../.assets/c4903bbe-6056-43f4-b17a-06c9d8af8a55.png" alt="" width="100%" />

4. Go to the Details tab and enter the Agreement Amount. The amount must be entered in the Agreement Currency.

 <img src="../.assets/746a766b-2fbd-43ba-b355-0ab6f3cf8049.png" alt="" width="100%" />

5. Go to the General tab and complete:

(1) Agreement Type - General  
(2) Payment Terms - enter the agreed payment terms  
(3) Select the agreed Price List  
(4) Change the Agreement Status to Approved  

   <img src="../.assets/dce7c194-9a23-4d0f-a5e1-26b011cf9957.png" alt="" width="100%" />

6. Click [Add] to save the Agreement.

After the Agreement is saved with Approved status, it can be selected in sales documents.  
As other sales documents referencing the Agreement are created, they are displayed in the Agreement on the Documents information tab:
