This step is part of the Production process.  
It records the issue/transfer of goods required for Production from the inventory warehouse to the production warehouse.

1. Open the list of **open** Inventory Transfer Requests.

2. Select the required document from the list and click [Choose].

<img src="../.assets/e3cf856d-97b7-4479-bbe9-8f0596e16027.png" alt="" width="100%" />

3. The System opens the list of requested items.

   <img src="../.assets/46d1217b-5a63-4d51-bf5c-8a4a2254ba2e.png" alt="" width="100%" />

4. Verify the warehouses and quantities in the rows. Click [Copy To] and select [Inventory Transfer] as the target document.

5. The System opens Inventory Transfer and prepopulates it with the request data. At this stage, the transfer document can be adjusted to reflect the actual quantity issued.

6.  
(1) Verify and, if necessary, adjust the document date.  
(2) Verify and, if necessary, adjust the quantity of each item.  
(3) Sign the document by selecting your record from the list.  
Click [Add] to save/post the data.  

As a result:  
The System transfers the goods between warehouses (in the example, from GN to FT).  
A Journal Entry is created: DR Inventory on Stock / CR Inventory on Stock.

   \  
   <img src="../.assets/963cea99-a19a-48ad-ab24-0666d04231be.png" alt="" width="100%" />

The inventory transfer / issue of goods to Production is complete.
