Business Partner ageing is represented in SAP by two reports available under Main Menu → Business Partners → Business Partner Reports → Ageing:

* Customer Receivables Ageing
* Vendor Liabilities Ageing

 <img src="../../.assets/2f2ccb34-d282-4493-92a1-d5cf05b64c34.png" alt="" width="100%" />

The reports use similar selection-criteria forms:  
 <img src="../../.assets/d2c63e40-7dcf-4280-9448-154c7af7c499.png" alt="" width="100%" />(1) Optionally, the report can be run for a specific Business Partner.  
(2) Allows transactions for specific control accounts to be selected.  
(3) Date as of which the report will be generated.  
(4) Defines how ageing periods are displayed. In the example, the table is split into Total Balance | Due within 30 | 40 | 90 | 120+ days.  
(5) Filters transactions by posting date.  
(6) Controls display of additional information in the report.  
(7) Used to show a combined balance for partners represented in the System both as Customer and Vendor. If balances exist on both sides, the report shows the net balance after combining receivables and payables.  

After configuring the criteria, click [OK].  
The report opens:  
 <img src="../../.assets/d159dcdc-2c53-41cb-8c5b-6fc40338b094.png" alt="" width="100%" />
(1) Report currency selection. Recalculation is performed as of the report date specified in the criteria.  
(2) Information about the transaction generating the balance.  
(3) Outstanding amount split by expected payment periods.  
(4) Source used to calculate ageing periods (Posting Date or Due Date).
