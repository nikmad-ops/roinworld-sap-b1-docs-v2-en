This step of the purchasing process starts when goods/materials physically arrive at the Company's warehouse.  
**Important: Posting a Goods Receipt PO records the goods into Company inventory. A Journal Entry is also created when the document is added. This means that a posted document cannot be edited. Corrections can only be made by creating a Goods Return (or cancelling the Goods Receipt PO).**

To record the receipt:

1. Open Goods Receipt PO. The System opens the form in Add Mode. From Main Menu → Purchasing - A/P → Goods Receipt PO.

2. Complete the document as follows:

 <img src="../.assets/fd0a6d8b-374f-4e38-aeb0-85ac200c996a.png" alt="" width="100%" />

(1) Enter/select the Vendor.  
(2) Enter the receipt date, reflecting the actual physical receipt date. All three dates should be the same.  
(3) Enter the Vendor delivery-note/reference number, which can later be used when communicating with the Vendor.  
(4) Click [Copy From].  

3. The System opens the list of **open** Purchase Orders created for the selected Vendor. Select the required Purchase Order and click [Choose].

(To select several Purchase Orders, hold [Shift]+[Ctrl].)

 <img src="../.assets/ed0ecf11-695c-4ccd-863d-06a04b303869.png" alt="" width="100%" />

4. The System opens the form for selecting data to copy from the Purchase Order into the Goods Receipt PO.

   <img src="../.assets/deb7847e-907c-4c17-bd47-436bd32507f6.png" alt="" width="100%" />

5. Select:

(1) 'Use Current Exchange Rate from The Exchange Rate Table'.  

(2) Select 'Customize' so the actual received quantity can be entered.  

6. The System opens the row-selection window.

All rows are highlighted in blue, meaning they will all be copied to the target document. If goods for a row were not received in full, clear the selection and choose the rows to copy by clicking them while holding [Ctrl].

 <img src="../.assets/ea008864-6a77-4a03-8283-351827de8745.png" alt="" width="100%" />

7. Enter the actual counted quantity received in column (1) [Quantity], and verify the [UoM Code].  
(2) Verify the [Whse] column - General Warehouse must be selected.  
(3) Verify that Key and Department dimensions are completed.  
After verification, click [Finish].

8. The System copies the selected rows and values from the Purchase Order(s) into the Goods Receipt PO.

 <img src="../.assets/45514fd7-7c31-4eb7-8c71-c535f802baf7.png" alt="" width="100%" />

9. Sign the document by selecting your signature in the Owner field.

10. Click [Add] / [Add & View] to post the document. At this point:

    * the goods are added to warehouse inventory;
    * a Journal Entry is created: DR Inventory On Stock / CR Goods Received Not Invoiced.

11. Print the document:

    * click (1) [Print] on the Tool Panel. This printout confirms receipt of goods for the Vendor;
    * to generate an electronic copy, click (2) [Preview] and save the layout as a PDF file.

<img src="../.assets/56a007fe-e1ba-48e9-84cb-28c37487ceee.png" alt="" width="100%" />

12. Sign the printed document and provide it to the Vendor.
